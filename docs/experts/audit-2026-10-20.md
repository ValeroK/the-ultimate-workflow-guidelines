## What was fixed during the audit

- `docs/experts/quality.md` and `docs/experts/security.md` did not exist.
- No `CHANGES.md` recorded the restructure; all six now do, each saying it is not measured.
- New skills had no selection tests (`event-schema-evolution`, `test-gates`, `eval-integrity`); added, with near misses and the cap. Upper-case `JUDGE` did not select `eval-integrity`; fixed.
- The playbook itself described the old shapes (an untrusted-input section, a defined empty result, `[G]` tags) and had no ledger step; brought up to date.
- Duplicates found between skills and removed: the `finally` rule (kept in `failure-handling`, cut from `python-craft`); published-package SemVer moved out of `schema-evolution` to `typescript-craft`.
- An unquoted figure (Spring Boot's executor queue) was tagged [T] instead of [R].

## What was fixed after the audit

- The protocol is now pasted into every expert prompt by code (it was only named, and smoke runs had shown experts not opening files they were told to).
- The developer's worked finding was a held-out corpus case; replaced, and a test refuses corpus ids and verbatim summaries in shipped files.
